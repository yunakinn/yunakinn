## Featured research

### [VLM Privacy & Confidence](https://github.com/yunakinn/vlm-privacy)

**Does reducing hallucination also reduce privacy risks?**

A study of sensitive-attribute inference with LLaVA-1.5-7B, comparing baseline decoding with VCD, M3ID, and ICD. Hallucination mitigation increased confidence in 17 of 18 comparisons, while accuracy and calibration changed inconsistently.

Conducted during the **2026 USTC Future Scientist Student Exchange Program (FUSEP)**.
